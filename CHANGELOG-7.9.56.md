## Hyper Eternity 7.9.56

### New
- **English in the settings app**: Settings → App language (Automatic, Português, English)
- **Comet progress bar (beta)**: the media player bar becomes a comet, with the cover color fading into a glowing white tip and a V-shaped tail that grows as the song plays. It moves smoothly and goes back to the original bar when paused. Thickness, tail, glow, pulse, sparks and colors are adjustable
- **Light ripple on track change**: like ColorOS 17, a light wave starts at the cover and spreads across the card (intensity up to 300%)
- **Vibrance on the artist name and the previous/next buttons (beta)**, with its own intensity

### Fixes
- Settings app: switches showed some features as on when they were actually off (HyperOS 4 report). The app now shows the module's real state

### Install
- Install over the previous version (same package and signature; your settings are kept)
- Restart the System UI or the device
